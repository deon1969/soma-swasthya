About the Practitioner

Avanthi Singh has spent her career helping people listen to what their bodies already know.

Trained in classical Ayurvedic principles, Avanthi works with each client to understand their unique constitution — their prakriti — and build a practical, sustainable path toward balance. Her approach blends traditional diagnostics with modern lifestyle realities: no extremes, no one-size-fits-all protocols, just care that fits the life you actually live.

Every consultation at Soma Swasthya begins with listening. From there, Avanthi builds a plan that may include dietary guidance, herbal support, daily rhythm (dinacharya), and seasonal adjustments — revisited and refined as you change.

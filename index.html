About the Practitioner

Avanthi Singh has spent her career helping people return to their body's own intelligence.

Holding an M.Sc. in Ayurveda & Integrative Medicine, Avanthi works with each client through pulse diagnosis (Nadi Vigyan), intuitive readings, and classical Ayurvedic assessment to understand their unique mind-body-spirit constitution. Her approach blends traditional diagnostics with modern lifestyle realities: no extremes, no one-size-fits-all protocols, just care that fits the life you actually live.

Every consultation at Soma Swasthya moves through the same rhythm: discover your constitution, rebalance your lifestyle and daily routines, restore vitality through herbal and Panchakarma support, and awaken deeper wellbeing through conscious living and meditation.
